## Description 
> AutoAid is a Mechanic and detailing app focusing on helping customers find certified automotive professionals who align with their specific needs. Whether they need a tune-up, bodywork, detailing, a paint job, or anything else they may need, they can find someone for them. The system supports customers seeking reliable vehicle care and also providers who want to offer their services. Finding experts, scheduling appointments, and locating everything a car needs are all made easier with AutoAid, all on one platform.

## App Functions
1. Customer (the user with the customer role):
    1. Create/modify customer profile - Register as a customer and find which mechanic can meet their vehicle's needs.
    2. View available services - Browse the app to look for the specialist that meets their needs (oil change, bodywork, paint job, etc.)and view their certification and specializations.
    3. Subscribe to available services - Book a recurring checkup or just a one-time service.
    4. Write reviews for subscribed services - Leave reviews on how well the mechanic did on their vehicle and if they would recommend them.
2. Provider (the user with the provider role):
    1. Create/modify/remove provider profile - Register as provider is able to show professions and add or remove there profile.
    2. Create services - Publish offerings and provide availability for customers.
    3. View customer statistics - Vehicle history, recents events, and common problems.
    4. Reply to reviews - Monitor success rate, recent customers, compatability with others.
