## Description 
> AutoAid is a Mechanic and detailing app focusing on helping customers find certified automotive professionals who align with their specific needs. Whether they need a tune-up, bodywork, detailing, a paint job, or anything else they may need, they can find someone for them. The system supports customers seeking reliable vehicle care and also providers who want to offer their services. Finding experts, scheduling appointments, and locating everything a car needs are all made easier with AutoAid, all on one platform.
